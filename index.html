Rise & Run Official Rules

Aim of the Game

Be the first player to play all of your cards. Match the top card of the discard pile based on your die roll.

Dice Roll Matching Rules

Roll 1 - No Luck!

Turn ends immediately. Cannot play card.

Roll 2 - Parallel

Same gradient $m$. Horizontal lines parallel to horizontal, vertical to vertical.

Roll 3 - Perpendicular

Gradients multiply to $-1$ ($m_1 \cdot m_2 = -1$). Horizontal & Vertical are perpendicular.

Roll 4 - Same y-intercept

Same $c$ value. Wildcard: $x=0$ matches any y-intercept!

Roll 5 - Same x-intercept

Same x-intercept point. Wildcard: $y=0$ matches any x-intercept!

Roll 6 - Drop Any Card!

Wildcard Roll: Drop any card of your choice.

Challenge Rule

If an incorrect card play is challenged: If invalid, player takes back card + draws 1 penalty card. If valid, challenger draws 1 card.
